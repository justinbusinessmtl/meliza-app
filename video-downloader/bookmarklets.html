Add the browser buttons

Drag each button below onto your bookmarks bar. After that, one click on any video page downloads it.

⬇ Video ♪ MP3 📝 Transcript

1. Show your bookmarks bar

Press ⌘ ⇧ B in Safari or Chrome.

2. Drag the three buttons onto it

Safari may ask you to name the bookmark: keep the name and click OK.

3. Use them

  • Open a video page, click ⬇ Video: you get an MP4 (720p, or 1080p if there's no 720p).
  • ♪ MP3 saves only the audio.
  • 📝 Transcript saves the text (.txt) and subtitles (.srt).
  • The first time, your browser asks to open “Video Downloader”. Click Allow (in Chrome, tick “Always allow” so it stops asking).

Safari button does nothing?

  1. Safari → Settings → Advanced → tick “Show features for web developers”.
  2. Safari → Settings → Developer → tick “Allow JavaScript from Smart Search Field”.
